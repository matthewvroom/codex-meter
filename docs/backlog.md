## Settings flyout at the bottom of the popover

**Status:** Proposed · November 16, 2026

In the 1.6.10 release candidate screenshot, the expanded Settings section reaches the bottom of the popover at the Updates heading. The update controls are below the visible edge.

Explore opening a dedicated Settings flyout from the bottom of the popover. Keep the creator credits and Portfolio and GitHub links easy to find, and make sure all settings and update controls are reachable on smaller displays, including when This Mac activity is expanded.
